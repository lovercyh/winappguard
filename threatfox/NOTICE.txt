Usage in WinAppGuard
--------------------
- full.csv is parsed locally into an IP / domain / file-hash index.
- Matching is alert-only; nothing is blocked from this data.
- threatfox.rules is optional and off by default; when enabled it is
  filtered by confidence and appended to the active Suricata rule set.
